### Hi, I'm Marc 👋

Mathematics and physics graduate ([UAB](https://www.uab.cat/), double degree) working on **machine learning grounded in mathematics and physics**: irregularly sampled time series, learning from simulations when labelled data are scarce, and the statistics of rare events, applied to astronomy.

Currently a financial risk analyst in credit risk at Deloitte (Barcelona), validating PD, LGD and credit-scoring models. Aiming for a master's and PhD in machine learning research.

#### Projects

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**light-curve-transformer**](https://github.com/marc-flores-catlla/light-curve-transformer) | Transformer that classifies irregularly sampled astronomical light curves into 25 variability classes, trained on synthetic curves with real telescope cadences and noise. Research internship at Scalai, being integrated into Light Bridges' platform for astronomers. *PyTorch* |
| [**extreme-event-forecasting**](https://github.com/marc-flores-catlla/extreme-event-forecasting) | BSc thesis in Mathematics: inter-event time models (Exponential, Weibull, Gamma regression) to forecast extreme solar radio bursts. Rejects the Poisson model and estimates a (18.8 ± 3.4)% ten-year probability of a burst above 10⁶ SFU. *Python, SciPy* |
| [Skyrmion ternary logic](https://marc-flores-catlla.github.io/#research) | BSc thesis in Physics: micromagnetic simulations in C (Landau–Lifshitz–Gilbert) of skyrmions and 2π-skyrmions as carriers for ternary logic gates. |
